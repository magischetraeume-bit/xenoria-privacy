Privacy Policy for Xenoria

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Xenoria is developed and published by Imre Creative Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Xenoria Android application.

We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide, maintain, improve and support the application.

1. Information stored on your device

Xenoria contains features such as a dream journal, dream-related tools, meditation functions, alarms, application settings and other personal customization options.

Much of the information created through these features is stored locally on your device.

This may include, for example:

  • dream journal entries;
  • dream titles and dates;
  • selected dream types;
  • custom dream types;
  • application preferences;
  • language settings;
  • meditation settings;
  • alarm settings;
  • selected journal appearance settings;
  • references to music files selected from your device.

Xenoria does not upload your personal music files to our servers. When you select your own meditation music from your device, Xenoria stores permission to access the selected file and plays it directly from your device through Android's file access system.

2. Dream content and AI analysis

Xenoria provides optional AI-powered dream analysis features.

AI processing occurs only when you actively request an AI analysis. In order to perform the requested analysis, the relevant dream content and information required for the analysis may be transmitted securely to our backend infrastructure and to an AI service provider used to generate the analysis.

Depending on the analysis requested, optional contextual information provided by the user, such as mood, sleep quality, selected experience type or other information entered for the analysis, may also be included in the request.

Dream content is not sent to the AI service simply because you write or save a dream. Transmission occurs only when an AI-powered feature requiring that content is used.

We do not use the content of your private dream journal for advertising.

3. Analytics

Xenoria uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the application is used and to improve its features and performance.

Analytics information may include events such as:

  • application sessions;
  • screens or features opened;
  • the application language being used;
  • whether certain features were started or completed;
  • dream save events;
  • AI analysis start, success or error events;
  • meditation timer usage;
  • meditation music usage;
  • interaction with rating or sharing functions.

Xenoria does not intentionally send the text or title of your dreams to Firebase Analytics.

4. Crash and diagnostic information

Xenoria uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to help identify crashes, unexpected errors and technical problems.

Crash reports may contain technical information such as device type, operating system version, application version, crash stack traces and diagnostic information related to the failure.

This information is used to improve application stability and fix technical problems.

5. Advertising

Versions of Xenoria that include advertising may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Depending on your location, preferences, consent choices and applicable law, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, advertisement interactions and other information permitted by your consent settings and applicable law.

Xenoria uses Google's consent management technology to request and manage advertising-related consent where required.

Users may be able to review or change applicable privacy and advertising choices through privacy settings provided inside the application.

The free version or free plan of Xenoria may include advertising. If an advertising-free plan or other advertising-free entitlement is active, Xenoria does not request advertisements for that advertising-free experience while the entitlement remains active.

6. Plans, purchases, subscriptions and AI credits

Xenoria may offer different access options, including a free plan, an advertising-free paid plan, premium or subscription-based features, and separately purchasable AI credits.

The free plan may include advertising. An advertising-free plan is intended to remove advertisements while the applicable entitlement remains active. Premium or subscription-based options may provide access to additional features or higher usage limits.

AI credits may be purchased separately and may be used to access AI-powered analysis features. AI credits are separate from advertising removal unless a specific offer explicitly states otherwise.

Purchases, subscriptions and other digital transactions are processed through Google Play Billing. Xenoria does not receive or store full payment card or bank account details.

Google Play may provide Xenoria with purchase-related information that is necessary to verify and manage digital purchases. This may include information such as the purchased product or subscription, purchase token, transaction status, subscription status, entitlement status, cancellation or refund status and purchase history information.

Purchase-related information may be processed by Xenoria and its backend infrastructure to validate transactions, restore purchases, maintain premium or advertising-free entitlements, manage AI credit balances, prevent fraud and provide the digital products or services purchased by the user.

7. Meditation music and Cloudinary

Xenoria may provide meditation music streamed from cloud storage infrastructure operated through Cloudinary.

When cloud-hosted meditation music is played, your device connects to Cloudinary's content delivery infrastructure to retrieve the requested media.

Xenoria may cache streamed meditation music locally on your device in order to reduce repeated downloads and improve playback performance.

8. Permissions

Xenoria may request Android permissions that are necessary for specific features.

Depending on the features you use, these may include permissions related to:

  • internet access;
  • notifications;
  • foreground media playback;
  • alarm functionality;
  • Do Not Disturb access when explicitly enabled by the user;
  • access to user-selected media or files.

Permissions are used only for the features for which they are required.

9. Third-party services

Xenoria may use third-party services to provide application functionality.

These may include:

  • Google Firebase Analytics;
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics;
  • Google AdMob;
  • Google User Messaging Platform or related consent services;
  • Google Play Billing;
  • Cloudinary;
  • AI infrastructure and AI service providers used for requested AI analysis.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

10. Data retention

Locally stored application data remains on your device until it is deleted through application functionality, removed through Android settings, or removed by uninstalling the application, subject to any device backup functionality enabled by your operating system.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the retention rules and privacy policies of those providers.

Purchase, subscription, entitlement and AI credit information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide purchased services, maintain entitlements, restore purchases, handle cancellations or refunds, prevent fraud and comply with applicable legal or accounting requirements.

11. Data deletion

You can delete locally stored dream entries and other supported content through Xenoria's available controls.

Uninstalling Xenoria normally removes locally stored application data from the device, subject to Android backup or restore functionality.

Xenoria does not currently provide user accounts or a central personal profile database from which users can request deletion of all locally stored application data. Locally stored content is managed directly on the user's device.

For privacy-related questions concerning information handled directly by Imre Creative Studio, you may contact us using the email address provided below.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical measures intended to protect information processed through Xenoria.

Information transmitted between Xenoria, its backend infrastructure and supported third-party services is transmitted using encrypted network connections where supported by the applicable service.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Children's privacy

Xenoria is not specifically designed as a service directed to children. The intended primary audience of Xenoria is adults.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through the application.

If you believe that information relating to a child has been provided inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

14. International processing

Some third-party service providers used by Xenoria may process information on servers located outside your country of residence.

Where applicable, such processing is subject to the safeguards and requirements provided by the relevant service provider and applicable law.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Xenoria's features, services, monetization options, third-party services or legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
